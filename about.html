About Marrow

Marrow is a web product for small brands and in-house teams that need to ship short-form ads without standing up a whole agency stack.

You chat with the desk. It writes copy, generates a video, and adds voice. The draft sits in your library. Publishing to TikTok, Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube, or X happens only after you connect that account and approve the specific piece.

We built it this way because auto-posting is how brands get burned. Marrow is a maker plus a gate, not a firehose.

What we will not do

We will not post to your TikTok without an explicit approve. We will not sell your content or tokens. We will not hide Privacy or Terms behind a login.
