How it works

Draft. Approve. Post.

Same loop every time. Marrow makes the work. You keep the last word.

01

Tell it the job

Brand, offer, and audience stay locked so the desk does not invent a fake company. One sentence is enough to start a draft.

02

Get a voiced cut

Video models generate the picture. ElevenLabs voices the line. Scene audio is on the first pass so nothing ships silent.

03

You hit approve

Then it can post to TikTok and other connected channels. Never before. You can disconnect TikTok at any time.

FAQ

Quick answers

Does Marrow post to TikTok without me?

No. Connecting TikTok lets Marrow publish only after you approve a specific draft. You can disconnect at any time.

Do I need an account to read this site?

No. This homepage, About, FAQ, Contact, Privacy Policy, and Terms of Service are public.
