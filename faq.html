FAQ

Questions

What is Marrow?

A web marketing desk. You describe a campaign. It drafts copy and short-form video. You approve before anything can post.

How does TikTok publishing work?

You connect a TikTok account with OAuth. Marrow stores tokens to publish videos you approve. Unaudited apps post as private (SELF_ONLY) until TikTok completes app review. You can disconnect anytime.

Can Marrow post without my approval?

No. Approve is the only publish path.

Is this website a login screen?

No. The homepage, About, FAQ, Contact, Privacy Policy, and Terms of Service are public. The signed-in desk is a separate app surface.

Where do I read the policies?

Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

How do I contact you?

See Contact or open a GitHub issue.
